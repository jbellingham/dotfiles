---
name: grill-me
description: Interview the user relentlessly about a plan or design until reaching shared understanding, resolving each branch of the decision tree. Use when user wants to stress-test a plan, get grilled on their design, or mentions "grill me".
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one by one.

Ask one question at a time. Put your recommended answer under it, and word the question so "yes" accepts it.

Finding facts is your job, not mine. When a question needs a fact from the code, tools or docs, send a subagent to find it. Keep asking the questions that don't wait on that fact.

Decisions are mine. The session is done when every branch is visited and nothing is silently assumed. Don't act on the plan until I confirm we've reached a shared understanding.
